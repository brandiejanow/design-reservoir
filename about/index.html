About

Design Reservoir is an independent public reference for design, research and education across STEAM, futures and cultural heritage.

It brings together institutions, academic sources, archives, methods and opportunities. The Design Atlas maps fields of design, while the Design Weeks index and Events Calendar track public programs. Research tools and other source types can be found through the filters in the main directory.

Independently curated by Brandie Janow. Entries link to their original sources. Inclusion is for reference and does not imply endorsement.
