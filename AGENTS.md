# brandiejanow/design-reservoir

This repository owns the independent international reference directory. Do not convert it into a personal paper archive, nor conflate curated third-party resources with individually authored research.

# Editorial instructions for this GitHub project

## Decide where each piece of work belongs
Before creating a page, dataset, research study, or repository, identify who did the work, when it began, its original project or institution, who owns the present study, and where its main publication should live. Do not put everything under Knowledge of Design because it concerns design. One work should have one main publication location. Relevant sites may link to that location.

- The personal website at brandiejanow.com covers the full professional career.
- The brandiejanow/research repository is the index of independent research by Brandie Janow, including work predating KoD and current work. A separate repository is reasonable for a large, continuing publication with its own data and versions; do not make a repository for every paper.
- The brandiejanow/Knowledge-of-Design repository records KoD's own research, annual editions, participants, talks, international programs, advisory work, focus groups, and KoD publications. Preserve this public history, people, places, and dates. Work before KoD remains credited to its actual source.
- The brandiejanow/Saudi-Creative-Ecosystem-Observatory repository contains Brandie's independent Saudi Creative Economy research, developed over many years before KoD. Preserve its own original chronology and research provenance. Kingdom Creatives is part of the wider personal Saudi history to examine later, not a license to relabel earlier sources.
- The brandiejanow/design-reservoir repository is a curated international directory, not a place to publish all personally authored papers.
- Rukun's Saudi Creative Index is separate and can use only source research actually undertaken through Rukun. Do not copy personal research into its dataset.

## Research authorship
Credit the researcher close to the title, normally once rather than throughout the prose. When supported by the record, use "Lead Researcher and Author: Brandie Janow" or "Research and writing: Brandie Janow." Identify additional researchers, speakers, participants, design contributors, and institutions for the roles they actually fulfilled. A speaker or advisory participant is not automatically the author of the research.

Keep a clear difference between (a) earlier independently written work, (b) a later KoD research program based partly on that work, and (c) a university or institutional program. Never imply that an institution adopted a proposal or developed its own plans because of earlier advice without direct evidence. Unpublished and confidential advisory documents remain private unless publication rights, consent, and sensitive content have been checked.

## Source and visual rules
Use original dated records and existing research workbooks. Clearly distinguish a proposal, a delivered activity, a coded observation, and a measured result. Explain the source, denominator, and method for charts. Do not invent response counts or use a category count as evidence of popularity or impact. Use appropriate charts, maps, timelines, relationship diagrams, and explanatory models when they clarify the subject. Follow each site's existing visual identity; KoD's colors and typography come from styles.css.

## Writing and release
Write directly in natural American English. Avoid inflated claims, business jargon, repeated author names, stock praise, contractions, em dashes, and the verb "shape" in any form. Avoid words including empower, vibrant, fusion, synergy, foster, treasure, trove, tapestry, delve, dive, hub, innovative, innovation, catalyst, unlock, passion, meaningful, unleash, nurture, tailored, grow, rich, gem, and tangible. Describe actual people, decisions, locations, materials, institutions, and evidence instead.

Preserve approved site structures and previously published history. Make targeted edits rather than broad redesigns unless requested. Verify links, source boundaries, dates, and public claims before each release.
