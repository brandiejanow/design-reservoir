## Archives and preservation

- [Digital Preservation Handbook (Digital Preservation Coalition)](https://www.dpconline.org/handbook). Publisher permits English-language reuse with required acknowledgements, excluding logos; detailed handbook conditions and third-party credits apply. Translation permission is requested separately.
- [Digital Curation Centre: Research Data Guidance](https://www.dcc.ac.uk/guidance). Guidance is free to access, but the site retains copyright unless explicitly stated otherwise. Check individual guide licenses before copying or adaptation.
- [IIIF: Standards and Cookbook](https://iiif.io/get-started/cookbook/). IIIF specifications use CC BY; supporting JSON examples, contexts and test fixtures are dedicated under CC0. These permissions do not license the images or documents displayed through IIIF.
- [UCLA Modern Endangered Archives Program (MEAP)](https://meap.library.ucla.edu/). Public guidance and openly accessible resulting collections do not imply unrestricted reuse of every item. Source rights, consent and grant publication requirements must be checked separately.
- [British Library Endangered Archives Programme](https://eap.bl.uk/). Free research access does not confer blanket reuse rights. Consult collection records, partner restrictions and source-item permissions before reproduction.
- [Mathaf Encyclopedia of Modern and Contemporary Arab Art](https://mathaf.org.qa/en/encyclopedia/). Free reading. Mathaf requires written permission for public image reproduction and directs users to external rights holders for non-Mathaf material; no blanket open license assigned.
- [VADS: Visual Arts Data Service](https://vads.ac.uk/). Provider permits noncommercial educational and research use. Images, underlying artworks and catalog records retain their owners' copyrights; other uses require contacting the relevant collection owner.
