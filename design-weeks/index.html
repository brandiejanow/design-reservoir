A comparative view

What are design weeks about?

No two design weeks serve exactly the same purpose. Some emphasize commerce, others local communities, education, experimentation, public life or international promotion. Examining these differences helps us understand what cities prioritize through design.

These are starting points for comparison, not fixed rankings or exclusive classifications. Individual festivals may span several areas, and annual themes can change.

Commerce & industry

Trade, product launches, showrooms and professional relationships. Dallas Design Week and 3daysofdesign offer useful starting examples.

Community & culture

Local creative participation, studios and cultural identity. Nashville Design Week brings different fields together through citywide programming.

Research & experimentation

Emerging materials, technology, speculative inquiry and student research. Dutch Design Week places these areas prominently in its program.

Education & professional learning

Talks, workshops, mentorship and career development. Dallas Design Week includes professional education alongside its commercial programming.

Public design & civic life

How design enters shared spaces and public experience. Dubai Design Week includes public installations and spatial interventions.

International promotion

Citywide design districts, major institutions and international audiences. London Design Festival is a useful example.
